1. Overview

This policy describes how Wait Thats Real Automation handles information when used to manage and publish content to the Facebook Page "Wait... That's Real?".

2. Information Processed

The application uses Meta platform permissions and access tokens to perform authorized Page-management and publishing actions. Depending on the authorized actions, Meta may provide Page identifiers, Page information, publishing status, and content-related information.

The automation may also process post text, image references, topic information, and publication status stored in its connected workflow and spreadsheet services.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate the authorized content-publishing workflow, prepare and publish Page content, and maintain publication records and workflow status.

5. Storage and Security

Access credentials and workflow data should be protected with appropriate access controls. Information may remain in the connected services for as long as needed to operate the workflow or comply with applicable obligations.
